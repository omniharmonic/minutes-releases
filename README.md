# Minutes

Meeting notes that stay yours. Minutes records meetings on your Mac or iPhone, transcribes them on device, separates speakers, and writes a summary you can trust to check. Nothing goes to a Minutes server, because there isn't one.

## Download for Mac

Get **Minutes.zip** from the [latest release](https://github.com/omniharmonic/minutes-releases/releases/latest), unzip it, and drag Minutes to Applications. Requires macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon. The app is notarized by Apple.

Minutes checks this repository for updates. On second launch it asks whether to check automatically; you can also choose **Minutes → Check for Updates…**. Every update is signed, and the app verifies the signature before installing it.
